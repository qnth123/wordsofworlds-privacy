Words of Worlds Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

This policy describes how the Android game Words of Worlds (com.qnth.wordsofworlds) handles information.

Game progress and backups

The game stores your puzzle progress, scores, coins, language and preferences on your device. This version has no sign-in or online leaderboard. You can export or import a progress backup in Settings. If you share a backup or save it to a cloud service, the destination you choose handles that copy under its own privacy policy. Android may also back up app data according to your device settings.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob for banner, full-screen and rewarded ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as your IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics. Google may use this information to serve and measure ads, estimate general location, and prevent fraud. Ad personalization depends on your choices and applicable settings.

Where applicable, you can review or change your choices in the game under Settings → Ad privacy options.

Retention and deletion

Game progress stays in the app's storage until you clear the app's data or remove the app. To remove saved copies as well, delete any files you exported and manage any Android backups separately. Google handles retention of advertising data under its own policies.

If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond and keep them as long as needed to handle your request or meet applicable obligations. You can contact us with questions or requests about your information, including deletion requests.
