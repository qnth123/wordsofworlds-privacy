Privacy Policy

Words of Worlds – Word Puzzle

Effective date: 22 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how QNTH Media Network LTD (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information in connection with the Words of Worlds – Word Puzzle mobile game (“the App”). It applies to the App when used on supported iOS and Android devices.

We aim to explain what information may be processed, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

2. Information and data that may be processed

The information processed can depend on the features enabled in the version of the App you use and your device settings. This may include:

  • App and device information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and technical identifiers.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers where permitted by your device settings and applicable consent requirements, ad impressions, and interactions with advertisements.
  • Diagnostics and performance: crash reports, error logs, and performance information used to maintain and improve the App.
  • Usage information: information about interactions with game features, such as screens or features used, where collected by the App or its service providers.

We do not use this policy to claim that every category above is collected in every version or on every device. Actual processing depends on the SDKs, configuration, platform permissions, and your choices.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers where available, IP address, ad delivery information, and interactions with ads to provide, measure, and help protect advertising services.

Advertising and measurement may be subject to your device settings, applicable consent requirements, and the choices presented in the App or by your operating system. On iOS, permission may be requested before accessing the device’s advertising identifier when required by Apple. You can also review advertising and privacy controls in your device settings.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. How information is used

Information described in this policy may be used to:

  • operate and provide the App and its game features;
  • display and measure advertisements, where enabled;
  • identify errors, prevent abuse, and maintain app security;
  • understand app performance and improve stability and user experience; and
  • meet legal obligations and respond to legitimate requests.

5. Sharing with service providers

We may allow service providers that support the App, such as advertising, analytics, hosting, and crash-reporting providers, to process information on our behalf or in accordance with their own privacy terms. Google AdMob is one such advertising service when ads are enabled.

We do not sell personal information in the ordinary meaning of selling it for money. Some privacy laws define certain advertising-related disclosures or sharing more broadly; where applicable, we will respect the rights and choices provided by those laws.

6. Data storage and retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including security, troubleshooting, legal, and operational needs. Retention periods can vary depending on the type of information and the service provider involved.

Game progress and settings may be stored on your device or handled by platform or service features, depending on the version of the App. Please do not assume that deleting the App will delete information held independently by a third-party service.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

You can use device-level privacy settings to manage advertising permissions and identifiers. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your request where permitted by law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate steps. Advertising shown in the App must be configured in accordance with the applicable platform rules and laws for the App’s declared audience.

10. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.
